KHEPITUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.0.0
DATE: 2026-03-31
STATUS: stable

KEY FEATURES
- Ad-Free Streaming: Enjoy unlimited video and music playback with zero advertisement interruptions.
- Background & Audio Playback: Continue listening to videos and music in the background or with your screen turned off.
- Picture-in-Picture (PiP): Multitask effortlessly with automatic and manual Picture-in-Picture video mode.
- High-Speed Downloads: Download video and audio files in your preferred resolutions for offline viewing.

KHEPITUBE MUSIC & SHORTS
- YouTube Music Integration: Explore trending tracks, albums, artist pages, curated playlists, and live lyrics.
- Music Recognition: Instantly identify songs playing around you with the built-in Shazam-like audio scanner.
- Vertical Shorts Feed: Browse YouTube Shorts with full playback controls, auto-scroll, and speed settings.
- Custom Equalizer: Adjust your audio output with equalizer presets and personalized frequency controls.

PRIVACY & SMART INTEGRATIONS
- SponsorBlock: Automatically skip sponsored segments, intros, outros, and self-promotions.
- DeArrow: Display clean, clickbait-free video titles and non-misleading thumbnails.
- Return YouTube Dislike: View public dislike counts on all videos.
- Local Storage & Privacy: Your watch history, playlists, and settings stay encrypted and stored 100% locally on your device.

CUSTOMIZATION & CONNECTIVITY
- Solid Opaque Navigation: Fast, modern bottom navigation bar with active tab indicators and direct access to Home, Shorts, Music, Subscriptions, and Library.
- Streamlined Settings: Access Look & Feel, Watching, Your Data, System, and About settings in a clean 5-category layout.
- Cross-Device Sync: Sync your history, subscriptions, and playlists seamlessly across phones, tablets, and Android TV.
- Auto Update Checker: Get instant notifications and 1-tap APK downloads directly from GitHub Releases.
