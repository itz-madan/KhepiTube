KHEPITUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.1.0
DATE: 2026-04-01
STATUS: stable

NEW FEATURES & ENHANCEMENTS
- YouTube Account Integration: Sign in with Google to sync your playlists, likes, and subscriptions in Library.
- Real-Time Trending Feed: Tap the center Flame button on the navigation bar to fetch YouTube's real-time trending videos.
- Minimal Stream Quality Selector: Cleaned up video resolution selection with merged, rounded single-row resolution choices.
- Background Audio Play: Easily switch video playback to background audio-only mode from the MiniPlayer.
- In-App Auto Updates: Auto-checks GitHub releases on startup with 1-tap in-app download and installation.
- Default Preferences: SponsorBlock is enabled by default, and Continue Watching on app open is turned off by default.
