Welcome to **KhepiTube v1.0.0** — a fast, lightweight, ad-free video, music, and local media client for Android, built with modern **Jetpack Compose** and **Material 3 Expressive UI**.

---

## 🌟 Major Highlights

### 🗑️ Complete Local Media Trash Bin Management
- **TopBar Badge Indicator**: Live trashed file count badge on the Trash Bin icon button in the Local Media top bar.
- **Inline Trash Bin Banner**: Top notification card on the Local Media screen with a 30-day auto-delete warning and 1-tap **Manage** button.
- **Multi-Select & Batch Operations**: Select individual or all trashed files to batch-restore or permanently delete.
- **Storage Summary & Category Filters**: Displays real-time storage size (`X items • Y MB total`) and category filter chips (`All`, `Videos`, `Music`).
- **Permanent Delete Safety Dialogs**: Material 3 confirmation dialogs before permanent file deletion to prevent accidental data loss.

### 🎧 1-Tap Background Audio Switcher
- **Headphones Overlay Button**: Added a dedicated 1-tap **Background Play / Play as Audio** overlay button on video playback screens.
- **Seamless Media Transition**: Switch video playback into background audio-only mode instantly without interrupting playback state or position.

### 🎨 Material 3 Expressive UI & Adaptive Layouts
- **Adaptive Screen Layouts**: Optimized for phones, tablets, and foldables with multi-pane support.
- **Flowing Grid & List View**: Easily toggle between list and grid views for local videos and folders.

### ⚡ High-Speed Extraction & Ad-Free Playback
- **Native InnerTube Client**: High-speed video/audio streaming with support up to 4K 60fps.
- **SponsorBlock & DeArrow**: Auto-skip sponsored segments, intros/outros, and replace clickbait titles/thumbnails.
- **Return YouTube Dislike**: View live public dislike counts on all videos.

### 🎵 YouTube Music & Shazam Scanner
- **YouTube Music Integration**: Explore trending music charts, artist albums, playlists, and synced lyrics.
- **Song Scanner**: 1-tap nearby audio scanner to identify songs playing in your environment.

### 🔒 100% Privacy & On-Device Storage
- **Zero Telemetry**: No account required, no tracking, no analytics.
- **Encrypted Local Storage**: All watch history, subscriptions, and downloads stay 100% on your device.
